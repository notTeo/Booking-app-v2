The app's navigation, on every signed-in page: the wordmark, Home (house icon, to the dashboard), then inside a shop the shop name and its grouped nav items, and Account in the footer (no Log out; that is on the Account page). Pages outside a shop (dashboard, account) keep the very same sidebar, so it never changes: its links lead to the user's shop (the one opened last when they are in several). Someone who is not in any shop gets only the wordmark, Home and Account. There is no top bar on desktop. 256px rail on 960px and up; 80px collapsed rail (icons only, the wordmark shortened to "Be"); a drawer with a scrim below 960px (the app shell switches at 640px, with a `navbar navbar--app` holding the wordmark and, on the right, the menu button; the drawer slides in from the right, under that button).

One item is current (`aria-current="page"`). Items are 44px tall with an icon and a label; collapsed items need `aria-label`. Badges show counts that need action (pending bookings), not totals. The drawer traps focus and closes on Esc, scrim tap and link click.

The rail's right edge is a drag handle (200px to 400px, remembered per device; double-click resets to 256px). Dragged really close to the edge, below 140px, it snaps to the collapsed rail, and dragging back out past that point expands it again. With the handle focused, Left at the minimum width collapses and Right expands.
